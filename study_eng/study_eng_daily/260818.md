# English Session — 20260818

**Q (Tutor):** When you report those numbers — productivity, profit, cost savings — how do you make sure executives actually trust them? Have you ever had someone push back on your figures?
*(수치를 보고할 때 임원들이 그걸 신뢰하게 만들려면 어떻게 하나요? 누군가 반박한 적 있나요?)*

**My answer:**
> Umm, that's key point. I show the concrete workflow process by using valid, appropriate method precisely.
*(구체적인 업무 프로세스를 타당한 방식으로 보여준다. 비용이 얼마나 감소했고 효율·생산성을 어떻게 높였는지 구체적·타당한 수치로 보여주는 게 중요하다.)*

**Corrected:**
> Umm, that's a key point. What I usually do is walk them through the concrete workflow, using solid, credible methods. I think it's important to show executives exactly how much costs went down and how we improved efficiency and productivity — backed by real, concrete, and reliable numbers.

**더 나은 답변 (Better answer):**
> Umm, that's a key point. What I usually do is walk them through the actual workflow step by step, and back everything up with hard numbers — exactly how much costs dropped, how much productivity improved, and how we measured it. When executives can see the methodology behind the figures, they're a lot more likely to trust them.

**핵심 포인트:**
- `that's key point` → `that's a key point` : 셀 수 있는 명사 앞 관사 a 필수.
- `I show the concrete workflow` → `I walk them through the concrete workflow` : `walk someone through`가 단계별 설명에 훨씬 프로페셔널.
- `using valid, appropriate method precisely` → `using solid, credible methods` : 형용사 과다. 정확한 단어 하나가 나음.
- 질문 뒷부분(반박 경험)은 답변에서 빠짐 → 다음 턴에서 보완.

## 단어/표현
* walk someone through ~ - ~를 단계별로 자세히 설명해주다
* back something up with data - ~를 데이터로 뒷받침하다
* hard numbers / concrete figures - 구체적이고 반박하기 어려운 수치
* a key point - 핵심 지점 (관사 a 주의)
* solid, credible methods - 탄탄하고 믿을 만한 방법
