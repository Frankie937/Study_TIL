# English Conversation Session — 20260812

주제: AI 도입 / 임원 보고 (Business English)

---

## Turn 1

**Q (Tutor):** Your company is rolling out AI tools across affiliates. What's the hardest part — the technology itself, or getting people to actually use it?
*(계열사 전반에 AI 도구를 도입 중인데, 가장 어려운 부분은 기술 자체인가요 아니면 사람들이 실제로 쓰게 만드는 것인가요?)*

**My answer:**
> Actually I think getting people to use it is more harder than the technology itself because most people have wanted to (익숙한 것을 하려는 관성) ... 새로운 것을 익히고 변화하는 것을 좋아하지 않는다.

**Corrected:**
> Actually, I'd say getting people to use it is harder than the technology itself, because most people tend to stick with what they're familiar with — they're not that eager to learn new things or adapt to change.

**핵심 포인트:**
- `more harder` → `harder` : harder가 이미 비교급. more를 또 붙이면 이중비교(콩글리시).
- `I think ~ because ... have wanted to` → `I'd say ~ because ... tend to` : 문장이 중간에 끊기고 시제 애매. `tend to` = ~하는 경향이 있다.
- 문장을 한국어로 끝내지 말고 영어로 마무리하는 연습 필요.

---

## Turn 2

**Q (Tutor):** Have you seen this kind of resistance firsthand in your own team — and if so, what actually worked to get people on board?
*(본인 팀에서 이런 저항을 직접 겪은 적 있나요? 있다면 사람들을 참여시키는 데 뭐가 효과 있었나요?)*

**My answer:**
> umm, yes, I felt that showing useful case for their real work is very important.
*(실제 업무에 AI가 쓰여 생산성을 높이는 케이스를 보여주는 것이 중요하다고 느꼈다.)*

**Corrected:**
> Yeah, I felt that showing real, practical use cases — ones where AI actually boosted people's productivity in their day-to-day work — was really important.

**더 나은 답변 (Better answer):**
> Yeah, I felt that showing real use cases was key — like, when we demoed how AI could cut a weekly report from three hours down to thirty minutes, that's when people actually started paying attention. Once they saw it move the needle on their own work, buy-in came a lot easier. Before that, most people just saw it as extra work they had to learn on top of their real job.

**핵심 포인트:**
- `showing useful case` → `showing real, practical use cases` : case는 보통 복수형, `use case`가 비즈니스 고정 표현.
- `for their real work` → `in their day-to-day work` : 직역 느낌 제거. day-to-day = 실무/일상 업무.
- `very important` → `really important` : 구어체에서 really가 더 자연스러움.
- 구체적 숫자(3시간→30분)를 넣으면 설득력 급상승.

---

## Turn 3

**Q (Tutor):** Let's shift gears a bit. When you're preparing an AI project report for executives, what's the biggest challenge — simplifying the technical content, or predicting what questions they'll ask?
*(주제를 바꿔서 — 임원 보고서 준비 시 가장 큰 어려움은 기술 내용을 쉽게 푸는 것인가요, 임원 질문을 예측하는 것인가요?)*

> 표현 설명: **Let's shift gears** = 주제/방향을 바꾸자 (자동차 기어 변경에서 온 관용구). 비슷한 표현: Moving on to... / Let's switch topics.

**My answer:**
> umm Actually I think the part of delivering AI skills more easily to executives is really important, so that's hardest point.
*(경영진에게 AI 기술을 더 쉽게 전달하는 것이 중요해서 그 부분이 제일 어렵다고 생각한다.)*

**Corrected:**
> Yeah, I think making AI concepts easy to understand for executives is really important, so that's actually the hardest part for me.

**더 나은 답변 (Better answer):**
> Yeah, I'd say the hardest part is breaking down complex AI concepts for executives without a technical background. It's not enough to just explain how the technology works — I have to translate it into terms they actually care about, like cost, risk, or ROI.

**핵심 포인트:**
- `delivering AI skills` → `making AI concepts easy to understand` : 전달하는 건 기술(skill)이 아니라 개념(concept).
- `hardest point` → `hardest part` : part가 자연스러운 짝. point는 논점/요점.
- 왜 어려운지 이유를 한 문장 더 붙이면 설득력 상승.
